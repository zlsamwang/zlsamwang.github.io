Publications

An Analysis of Approaches to Goldbach's and De Polignac's Conjectures and Their Interconnections

2025

Zilong Wang · Highlights in Science, Engineering and Technology, Vol. 140 · 3rd International Conference on Algorithms, Mathematical Modeling and Machinery Processing (AMMMP 2025)

Reviews the major analytical approaches to Goldbach's and de Polignac's conjectures, including Chen's theorem, the Hardy–Littlewood Circle Method, and the GPY sieve for bounded prime gaps. Critically shows a 2014 purported proof of de Polignac's conjecture to be logically unsound, and computationally verifies both conjectures for even integers up to 10,000.

Correlation Between Fishing Yield and Microplastic Levels Across Nations

2024

Zilong Wang · Proceedings of the 2nd International Conference on Mathematical Physics and Computational Simulation

Used a mixed-effect model (with ReML estimation) on data from 100 nations spanning 1990–2021 to show that a one-piece-per-cubic-meter increase in microplastic levels corresponds to a 65–100 metric tonne decline in fishing yield (95% CI), a relationship consistent across ~93% of coastal nations studied.

Reproduction of Two Studies Discussing Bias: Jury Selection Bias and Diving2000 Findings

2024

Jiaqi Guo, Yunhao Mai, Chen Wang, Chentong Hao, Zilong Wang (co-first authors) · Proceedings of the 3rd International Conference on Computing Innovation and Applied Physics

Reproduced two influential studies using t-tests and permutation tests: found no statistically significant evidence of bias against education in jury selection, but confirmed nationalistic judging bias among several judges in the 2000 Olympic diving competition.
